\section{Ablation}
\label{sec:ablation}

Our recipe changes several parts of a standard time-series foundation
model. Table~\ref{tab:ablation} lists these changes. Each ablation
starts from our width-384 model of Section~\ref{sec:experiments}, the
reference, and replaces one change with its standard counterpart. The other settings stay the same. Each run trains for
$166{,}000$ steps, then a new forecast head trains on its backbone
(Section~\ref{sec:forecast-head}). The scores of the ablations will
come in a later version of this report.

$^{*}$Instance normalisation, as in RevIN~\citep{kim2022revin} and
Moirai~\citep{woo2024moirai}, needs a context: one mean and one standard
deviation over the whole window would read the targets
(Section~\ref{sec:normalisation}). Each series draws a context of one
patch to half of its window. One mean and one standard deviation over
the context normalise the whole window, and the losses keep only the
anchors whose next patch comes after the context.
