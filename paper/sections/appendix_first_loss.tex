\section{The first version of the model and its loss}
\label{sec:appendix-first-loss}

The ARMA and random-walk experiments of Section~\ref{sec:experiments}
use the first version of the model. It reads a series of shape
$T \times C \times F$: $T$ time steps, $C$ channels and $F$ features
for each step. Its latents have the size $H$. This version has no
encoder transformer: the forecaster reads the patch embeddings $e$
directly, and its target is the patch embedding of the next patch. The patch
embeddings and the forecasts lie on the unit sphere. The only loss is
an InfoNCE loss~\citep{oord2018cpc}, and this appendix defines it.

For two unit-norm latents we score their similarity by their inner
product, which is the cosine of the angle between them.

We index each latent and each forecast by the batch element
$b \in B$, the patch position $t \in T_p$, and the channel $c \in C$.
For every anchor $(b, t, c)$, the positive pair is the forecast
$f[b, t, c]$ paired with the embedding of the next patch
$e[b, t+1, c]$.

The negative set is built by varying anchors along three independent
axes: cross-temporal ($t \neq t'$), cross-channel ($c \neq c'$), and
cross-batch ($b \neq b'$). The loss uses three specific pair families.
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Cross-temporal} ($\mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{ct}}$): two
  embeddings from the same series and channel at consecutive
  times, $\big(e[b, t, c],\, e[b, t+1, c]\big)$. Per anchor, $1$
  candidate.
\item \textbf{Cross-channel} ($\mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{cc}}$): two
  embeddings from the same series at the same time, with
  different channels, $\big(e[b, t, c],\, e[b, t, c']\big)$ for
  $c' \neq c$. Per anchor, $C - 1$ candidates.
\item \textbf{Cross-batch} ($\mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{cb}}$): the
  embedding of the next patch of one series paired with the forecast
  of a different series at the same prediction position,
  $\big(e[b, t+1, c],\, f[b', t, c]\big)$ for $b' \neq b$. Per anchor,
  $B - 1$ candidates.
\end{itemize}

For unit-norm $u, v \in \mathbb{R}^H$, write the temperature-scaled
exponential of the inner product as
\[
  \sigma_\tau(u, v) \;=\; \exp\!\big(\langle u, v \rangle / \tau\big).
\]
The positive score at anchor $(b, t, c)$ is
\[
  \mathcal{P}_+(b, t, c) \;=\;
  \sigma_\tau\!\big(f[b, t, c],\, e[b, t+1, c]\big),
\]
and the three negative sums at position $(t, c)$ are
\begin{align*}
  \mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{ct}}(t, c)
    &\;=\; \sum_{b \in B}
      \sigma_\tau\!\big(e[b, t, c],\; e[b, t+1, c]\big),\\
  \mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{cc}}(t, c)
    &\;=\; \sum_{b \in B} \, \sum_{c' \neq c}
      \sigma_\tau\!\big(e[b, t, c],\; e[b, t, c']\big),\\
  \mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{cb}}(t, c)
    &\;=\; \sum_{b \neq b'}
      \sigma_\tau\!\big(e[b, t+1, c],\; f[b', t, c]\big).
\end{align*}
The contribution of the anchor $(b, t, c)$ to the loss is
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L}_{b, t, c}
  \;=\; -\log \frac{\mathcal{P}_+(b, t, c)}
  {\mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{ct}}(t, c) \;+\;
   \mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{cc}}(t, c) \;+\;
   \mathcal{N}_{\mathrm{cb}}(t, c)}.
  \label{eq:loss-anchor}
\end{equation}
The denominator depends only on $(t, c)$: every anchor at the same
position shares it. The mini-batch loss is
the average over all anchors,
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal{L} \;=\; \frac{1}{N}
  \sum_{b \in B} \sum_{t} \sum_{c \in C}
  \mathcal{L}_{b, t, c},
  \qquad N = B \cdot (T_p - 1) \cdot C.
  \label{eq:loss-batch}
\end{equation}
The cross-batch set is quadratic in $B$ and dominates the negatives.
