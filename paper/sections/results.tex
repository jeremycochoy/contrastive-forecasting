\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

Table~\ref{tab:gift-eval} gives the GM-MASE of the three models of
Section~\ref{sec:experiments} after $166{,}000$ steps. At width $64$, the
Moirai-style model scores $1.0090$ and our model scores $1.0164$. The
Moirai-style model at width $384$ scores $0.9362$. The Moirai-style
model at width $64$ has one patch embedding and one output head for
each of its five patch sizes, so it has $3.3$ times the parameters of
our model. Its learning rate decays to $0$ at the last step, and ours
stays at $10^{-3}$. Figure~\ref{fig:gift-radar} gives the relative
MASE of each GIFT-Eval dataset.

  \label{tab:gift-eval}

  \end{tabular}
