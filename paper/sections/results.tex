\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

Table~\ref{tab:gift-eval} gives the GM-MASE of the four models of
Section~\ref{sec:experiments} after $166{,}000$ steps. At width $64$,
our model scores $1.0057$ and the Moirai-style model scores $1.0090$.
At width $384$, our model scores $0.9401$ and the Moirai-style model
scores $0.9362$. The Moirai-style model at width $64$ has one patch
embedding and one output head for each of its five patch sizes, so it
has $3.3$ times the parameters of our model.

Figure~\ref{fig:gm-curves} gives the GM-MASE along training. Our
learning rate is constant, then cools down; the Moirai-style models
follow a cosine schedule to $0$. At both widths, our model stays close
to the Moirai-style model. After the cooldown it is below it at width
$64$, and within $0.004$ of it at width $384$. Figure~\ref{fig:gift-radar} gives the
relative MASE of each GIFT-Eval dataset.
