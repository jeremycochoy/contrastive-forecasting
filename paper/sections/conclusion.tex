\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The experiments of Section~\ref{sec:experiments} verify that the
contrastive forecasting backbone, trained in latent space with
explicit InfoNCE negatives, learns enough about the data-generating
process to let a small recovery head, fitted on the frozen latents,
recover the ARMA parameters. The head reaches an improvement ratio
of $8.34$ on the 2x2 configuration and $6.96$ on the 2x4. Within the
bounds of synthetic ARMA data, the latents encode the parameters of
the underlying stochastic process.

The synthetic distribution is also the main limit of these results.
ARMA captures only one slice of what real time series can look like,
so the recovery numbers do not say anything about how the same
backbone behaves on observed data. The natural next step is to
evaluate it on a general time-series forecasting benchmark.
