\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Inspired by the JEPA principle of learning an abstract representation
rather than the raw values of the input (the pixels of an image, the
samples of a time series), we have trained a forecasting model
directly in latent space, in an unsupervised manner
(Section~\ref{sec:method}). A small head, trained on the frozen model,
maps its latent forecasts back to values.

Section~\ref{sec:results} gives a first evaluation of this model on
the GIFT-Eval benchmark. After $166{,}000$ steps, our model
scores a GM-MASE of $1.0164$, and a Moirai-style value-space model of
the same width scores $1.0090$. The ablations of
Section~\ref{sec:ablation} will measure the effect of each part of
our recipe.
