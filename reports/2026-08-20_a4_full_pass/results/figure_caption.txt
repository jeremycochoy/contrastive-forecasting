The ribbon is ±0.0029, one pooled head-seed standard deviation over 6 (stop, head) rows of 3 draws each. Measured ranges: 0.0018, 0.0038, 0.0042, 0.0062, 0.0064, 0.0087.
A standard deviation from 3 draws carries about 40% relative uncertainty, so read the range, not the ribbon edge.
The ribbon pools the student and the teacher into one number, so it assumes the two heads share one spread.
Draws exist at 200k, 300k, 450k only. At 40k, 100k the ribbon is an extrapolation.
The three hollow points are #373's. 40k comes from the cf373_r2 tree, 100k and 200k come from cf373_r3, and the 200k checkpoint file carries an _r2_ infix from the round that wrote it.
