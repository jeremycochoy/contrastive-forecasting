What `teacher_delta_bb100k_bb200k.csv` measures
===============================================

This file was called `null_frozen_teacher.csv`. That name was wrong and the
comparison it holds is NOT a null. The rename is the correction.

WHAT IT MEASURES
  The paired per-config change in GM-Relative MASE from the teacher head on
  the 100,000-step backbone to the teacher head on the 200,000-step backbone.
  The resampling unit is the DATASET, over the 97 GIFT-Eval configs.
  Producer: `reports/2026-08-08_rollout_depth/scripts/paired_bootstrap.py`.

WHAT IT DOES NOT MEASURE
  Repeatability. The two ends are two DIFFERENT models.

WHY THEY ARE DIFFERENT MODELS
  The teacher ENCODER freezes at step 100,000, because `--ema-tau-ramp-steps
  100000` takes the EMA momentum to exactly 1.0. `teacher_move_100k_200k.json`
  confirms it: 0 of 74 teacher tensors move.

  The teacher HEAD does not freeze. It loads 110 tensors. 74 come from a
  `teacher_*` key. 36 stay the student's, and 32 of those 36 move between
  these two checkpoints (`teacher_head_inputs_100k_200k.json`). The latents
  the head reads move with them: encoder latents rel L2 1.362e-02, forecaster
  latents rel L2 1.013e-01.

  The line of code that does it:

      src/checkpoint.py:266    out = dict(state_dict)

  `prepare_backbone_state_dict` starts from the full STUDENT state dict. The
  `encoder_source='teacher'` branch then overwrites two prefixes only,
  `teacher_input_to_latent.*` and `teacher_encoder_layers.*`
  (`_TEACHER_PROMOTIONS`, src/checkpoint.py:230). Every key those two do not
  name keeps the student's tensor.

THE NUMBERS
  label                       subset       n   delta     interval        p_improved
  teacher_bb100k_to_bb200k    all         97  -0.0046  [-0.0199, 0.0123]  0.711
  teacher_bb100k_to_bb200k    short       55   0.0150  [-0.0005, 0.0340]  0.029
  teacher_bb100k_to_bb200k    medium_long 42  -0.0381  [-0.0713, -0.0091] 0.996

  Read the `all` row. The other two are subsets picked after the numbers were
  seen.

  The aggregate change of -0.0046 is a measured change between two models. It
  is not a repeatability draw and it is not a noise floor. Do not read it as
  either.

WHERE THE NOISE BAND COMES FROM INSTEAD
  `head_band.csv`. Three head seeds on ONE backbone, at 200,000 steps:
    student   1.0660  1.0652  1.0642   range 0.0018
    teacher   1.0828  1.0809  1.0764   range 0.0064
  That is the band the report reads.
